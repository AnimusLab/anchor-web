1

Definitions

Interpretation

"Anchor Platform" means the AnimusLab governance software and services described in the Order Form, comprising Static Codebase Governance and Runtime AI Governance.

"Hub" means the isolated, dedicated deployment node provisioned for the Customer in the region stated in the Order Form.

"Clearance" means a named-user entitlement at a stated tier (L1 Developer, L1 Standard Auditor, L2 Project Lead, L2 Cross-Hub Auditor, L3 Hub Manager).

"Governed AI Model Slot" means a metered entitlement permitting one AI model or model endpoint to be placed under Runtime AI Governance.

"Decision Audit Chain" or "DAC" means the append-only, cryptographically linked record of governance decisions produced by the Hub.

"State Hash" means a fixed-length digest derived from runtime state, computed in the Customer's environment.

"Compliance Digest" means a compact summary of governance outcomes that contains no raw prompts, model weights, source code or personal data.

"Customer Data" means all data and content the Customer or its users submit to or process through the Anchor Platform, other than State Hashes and Compliance Digests.

"Entitlement Manifest" means the signed record of purchased capacity issued on settlement.

"Order Form", "Effective Date", "Term" and "Fees" have the meanings given in the Order Form.

Division of duty

4

Data architecture and confidentiality of Customer Data

Scoped commitment

4.1 Data minimisation by design. The Anchor Platform is designed so that raw prompts, model outputs, model weights, proprietary source code and Customer personal data remain within the Customer's environment. The Hub receives State Hashes and Compliance Digests.

4.2 Scope of the commitment. AnimusLab will not access, copy or store Customer Data outside the Customer's environment except (a) as the Customer expressly configures, (b) as strictly necessary to provide support at the Customer's written request, or (c) as required by law, in which case AnimusLab will give prior notice where lawful. This is a scoped commitment, not an unconditional representation that AnimusLab can never receive any data.

4.3 Ownership. As between the parties, the Customer owns Customer Data and all outputs generated from it. AnimusLab owns the Anchor Platform, its improvements and its documentation. Feedback may be used by AnimusLab without obligation, provided it does not disclose the Customer's confidential information.

4.4 Telemetry. Operational metrics that contain no Customer Data may be used to operate and improve the service. The Security Schedule lists the categories collected.

4.5 Personal data is governed by the DPA.
