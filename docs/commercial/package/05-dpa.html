Data Processing Agreement

Applies to the extent AnimusLab processes personal data on the Customer's behalf under UK GDPR, EU GDPR or equivalent law. The DPA prevails over the MSA as to personal data.

1

Roles and scope

Controller / processor

1.1 The Customer is the controller and AnimusLab is the processor for personal data processed under the MSA.

1.2 Description of processing (Annex 1). Subject matter: governance, audit and administration of the Anchor Platform. Duration: the Term plus retention in Section 5. Nature: hosting, storage, signing and transmission of governance records and user account data. Data subjects: the Customer's Clearance holders and administrators. Categories of personal data: names, work email addresses, role, authentication metadata, activity logs and support correspondence. By design, the Platform does not process end-user prompt content or model inputs and outputs. If the Customer configures it to do so, the Customer will tell AnimusLab first and the parties will update Annex 1.

2

Processor obligations

Documented instructions

AnimusLab will:

  • (a) process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are the MSA, Order Form and DPA, unless law requires otherwise (in which case it will notify the Customer where lawful);
  • (b) ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality;
  • (c) implement the technical and organisational measures in the Security Schedule (Annex 2);
  • (d) assist the Customer with data subject requests, security, breach notification, impact assessments and consultations with regulators, taking into account the nature of processing;
  • (e) delete or return personal data at the Customer's choice at the end of the services, subject to Section 5;
  • (f) make available information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow audits under Section 7.
3

Sub-processors

Authorisation

3.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in Annex 3 [hosting provider, email provider, support tooling and so on, to be completed].

3.2 AnimusLab will give at least [30] days' notice of new or replacement sub-processors. The Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds; if not resolved, the Customer may terminate the affected service with a pro-rated refund.

3.3 AnimusLab will bind sub-processors to equivalent obligations and remains liable for their performance.

4

International transfers

SCCs / UK Addendum

4.1 The Hub is hosted in [the EU-West region stated in the Order Form]. Personal data will not be transferred outside that region except as stated in Annex 3 or as the Customer instructs.

4.2 AnimusLab is established outside the UK and EEA [India]. Where personal data is accessed from or transferred to a country without an adequacy decision (for example, remote support access), the parties incorporate the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2, controller to processor) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, with their annexes populated from this DPA. [Counsel to confirm the current mechanism, including any adequacy decisions, and complete a transfer risk assessment.]

5

Retention, erasure and the audit chain

Immutability vs erasure

5.1 Account and user personal data is deleted or returned within [60] days after the end of the services, then purged from backups within [90] days.

5.2 Immutable records. DAC entries are designed to contain no directly identifying personal data. Where an entry can be linked to a person only through data held in the Customer's environment, the Customer can satisfy an erasure request by deleting that link data. If personal data is found in a DAC entry despite MSA Section 12.2, AnimusLab will cooperate to remove or cryptographically sever the link, record the action, and document how chain integrity remains verifiable. [Technical design to be confirmed by the engineering team.]

6

Security and personal data breach

Notification

6.1 AnimusLab will notify the Customer without undue delay and in any case within [48] hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with the information in the Security Schedule, and will provide updates as they become available.

6.2 AnimusLab will take reasonable steps to contain and remediate and will assist the Customer with its notifications to regulators and data subjects.

7

Audit

Third-party reports first

On reasonable notice, no more than [once per 12 months] absent a breach or regulator demand, the Customer may audit AnimusLab's compliance with this DPA. AnimusLab may satisfy the request first by providing third-party reports (for example SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification, once obtained) and answering a security questionnaire. On-site audits are limited to what is reasonably necessary and are subject to confidentiality.

8

Liability and precedence

DPA prevails for personal data

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the MSA (including any super-cap). The DPA prevails over the MSA for personal data matters.
