Acceptable Use Policy

Usage boundaries for the Anchor Platform, including static codebase governance and runtime AI governance.
Outline — to be completed Fair use applies
1

Permitted use

Internal business purposes
  • Govern the Customer's own repositories, AI models and model endpoints for internal business purposes.
  • Generate compliance evidence, audit records and governance summaries for the Customer's own regulators and auditors.
  • Grant Clearances to the Customer's personnel and named contractors within the purchased quantities.
2

Prohibited use

Hard limits
  • No reverse engineering, extraction of source, or circumvention of entitlement, signing or metering controls, except where law permits.
  • No resale, sublicensing, or provision of the Platform as a service to third parties.
  • No use to build or train a competing product.
  • No processing of unlawful content, or use in a way that violates sanctions, export-control or anti-corruption law.
  • No attempt to access another customer's Hub, data or audit chain.
3

Runtime AI governance boundaries

Model slots
  • Each distinct governed AI system consumes one Governed AI Model Slot. Binding a model to multiple projects does not consume additional slots.
  • Models in PENDING, ACTIVE or QUARANTINED status consume capacity; ARCHIVED and REJECTED models do not.
  • Registering a model beyond purchased capacity requires an Order Form amendment. Overages are handled under MSA Section 5.
4

Static codebase governance and fair use

Unrestricted, within reason
  • Repository registration and static scans are unrestricted for the Customer's own codebases, subject to fair use of [X scans per month / Y GB of source analysed per month].
  • Scanning third-party code the Customer is not licensed to analyse is prohibited.
  • AnimusLab may apply reasonable technical limits to protect platform stability, with notice where practicable.
5

Enforcement

Suspension

AnimusLab may suspend access for a material breach of this AUP that is not cured within [10] days of notice, or immediately where there is an imminent security or legal risk. Suspension will be limited to the affected service and lifted promptly once the issue is resolved. Suspension does not relieve the Customer of payment obligations.
