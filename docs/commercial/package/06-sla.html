1

Scope

Covered services

This SLA covers the Hub and the Anchor Platform services provided under the Order Form, excluding Customer-side agents and integrations running in the Customer's environment.

2

Availability commitment

Measurement

2.1 Monthly Uptime Percentage = (total minutes in the month − minutes of Downtime) ÷ total minutes in the month × 100.

2.2 Target: [99.9%] for the Hub management plane and audit chain ingestion, measured per calendar month.

2.3 Downtime means the Hub is unavailable to the Customer, as measured by AnimusLab's monitoring and verified against Customer-reported incidents. It excludes:

  • scheduled maintenance announced at least [5] business days in advance, capped at [8] hours per month;
  • emergency security patching, capped at [4] hours per month, with notice as soon as practicable;
  • force majeure, Customer-caused failures, Customer-side network or configuration issues and third-party systems the Customer controls;
  • suspension under the MSA.

2.4 Fail-closed behaviour. If the Customer configures runtime enforcement to fail closed when the Hub is unreachable, the resulting blocks of governed AI calls are a result of the Customer's configuration and are not a separate SLA breach, but Downtime is still measured. AnimusLab will document recommended fail-open and fail-closed configurations, and the Customer decides which to use.

Chronic failure

Termination right

If Monthly Uptime falls below the target in [two consecutive months or three months in any 12-month period], the Customer may terminate the affected services by written notice within [30] days and receive a pro-rated refund of prepaid Fees for the remainder of the Term.
