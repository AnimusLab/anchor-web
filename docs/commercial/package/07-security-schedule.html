Security Schedule

Technical and organisational measures for the Anchor Platform. This is an outline: complete each item with the engineering team before representing any control or certification to a customer.

1

Data minimisation and architecture

By design
  • Raw prompts, model outputs, model weights, proprietary source and Customer personal data remain in the Customer's environment by default.
  • The Hub receives State Hashes and Compliance Digests; the categories are listed in the DPA Annex 1.
  • DAC entries contain hashes and non-personal digests. The parties agree not to place personal data in DAC entries.
2

Encryption

In transit and at rest
  • All network traffic uses TLS [1.2 minimum, 1.3 preferred] with modern cipher suites.
  • Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. Key management: [KMS/HSM details to be completed].
  • Entitlement manifests are signed with Ed25519; private keys are held in [HSM/KMS] and rotated [annually].
3

Access control

Least privilege
  • Named-user Clearances; no shared accounts. Multi-factor authentication is required for administrative access.
  • Role-based access control with the tiers defined in the MSA; production access is logged and reviewed [quarterly].
  • Joiner/mover/leaver process with [24-hour] revocation on termination.
4

Logging, monitoring and incident response

Detect and respond
  • Centralised, tamper-evident logging of administrative actions and authentication events.
  • 24×7 monitoring for [S1] events, or [business-hours] monitoring until 24×7 staffing exists.
  • Documented incident response plan with roles, escalation and customer notification within [48] hours of a personal data breach.
  • Post-incident reviews for S1 incidents within [5] business days.
5

Backup, resilience and recovery

RPO / RTO
  • Encrypted backups in the same region as the Hub unless the Customer agrees otherwise.
  • RPO [≤ 1 hour] and RTO [≤ 4 hours] for audit chain data and the Hub.
  • Disaster recovery tested at least [annually]; summaries available on request.
6

Vulnerability management

Assurance
  • Dependency and container scanning in CI; critical vulnerabilities remediated within [7] days.
  • Independent penetration test at least [annually] and after material architecture changes.
  • Secure SDLC with code review and segregation of duties. [Tooling and evidence to be completed.]
7

Certifications and reports

  • SOC 2 Type II: [not yet obtained / report date].
  • ISO/IEC 27001: [not yet obtained / certificate number].
  • Cyber insurance: [insurer and limit].
